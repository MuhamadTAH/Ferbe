## Pedagogical Progression Templates
Every bespoke lesson follows the 10-screen architecture established in Lesson 1.1:
1. **Word Anchors (`word_anchors`)**: Lexicon introduction with transliteration, pronunciation badges, and audio.
2. **Acoustic Match (`acoustic_match`)**: Sound to spelling discrimination with audio-only playback and English tiles.
3. **Analytic Breakdown (`analytic_breakdown`)**: Multi-row lexical and grammatical deconstruction with connected fluent speech on the final row.
4. **The Echo Mic (`echo_mic`)**: Oral production rehearsal with native audio model, transliteration guide, and speech recognition.
5. **Status Adjectives Bank (`status_bank`)**: Frame-based sentence creation and vocabulary expansion.
6. **Slot-and-Filler (`slot_filler`)**: Syntax and lexical slot-filling with inline validation.
7. **Social Logic Match (`response_pairing`)**: Two-column conversational matching (initiations vs. natural rejoinders).
8. **Pragmatic Anchor (`bounce_back_anchor`)**: Core conversational idiomatic function identification.
9. **Interactive Chat Simulation (`chat_dialogue`)**: Contextual messaging simulation with partner bubble and selective draft choices.
10. **Capstone Spoken Gate (`capstone_spoken`)**: Timed forced spoken production evaluating acoustic accuracy $\ge 75\%$ before awarding XP and unlocking the next milestone.

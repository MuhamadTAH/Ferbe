# Lesson 1.7: Boss Checkpoint (The Street Dialogue)

**Objective:** Zero-hint, unassisted spoken roleplay integrating Theme A (Social Ping-Pong) and Theme B (Opinions & Distance). Pass with voice to unlock Unit 2.
**Duration:** ~2.5 minutes (8 screens)

---

## Screen 1: Scenario Briefing (The Challenge)
**Type:** Context Setup & Calibration

**UI Elements:**
- Animated avatar standing on a street corner
- Mission banner: Have a real conversation. No hints. No subtitles.
- Hardware check indicator: Green microphone status dot

**Audio & Mechanics:** System audio chimes: "Get ready. Respond naturally into the mic." User taps Start Conversation to engage the roleplay engine.

---

## Screen 2: Turn 1 — The Opening Handshake
**Type:** Spoken Response Under Pressure (3s)

**UI Elements:**
- Avatar waves in real-time. Speech bubble shows an active audio waveform (zero text).
- Red 3-second countdown ring around the live microphone.

**Audio & Mechanics:**
- Avatar speaks: "Hey! How are you?"
- Mic opens immediately with 3-second limit
- User must produce a valid status response: "I'm good, thank you", "I'm cool, thank you", or "I'm great, thank you"
- Engine evaluates speech latency and acoustic accuracy.

---

## Screen 3: Turn 2 — The Counter-Question
**Type:** Conversational Initiative (3s)

**UI Elements:**
- Avatar smiles and replies via audio: "I'm fine, thank you!"
- Prompt banner flashes: Keep the conversation going!
- Live mic with 3-second countdown

**Audio & Mechanics:** User must immediately fire the bounce-back question: "What about you?" Failure to ask within 3 seconds registers a dead air penalty.

---

## Screen 4: Turn 3 — The Agreement Reflex
**Type:** Conversational Agreement (3s)

**UI Elements:**
- Visual cue: An icon of a steaming plate of Dolma beside the avatar
- Live mic with 3-second countdown

**Audio & Mechanics:** Avatar speaks: "I like Dolma, what about you?" Mic activates instantly. User must say: "Me too!" (or state a valid preference like "I like BMW") before the clock hits zero.

---

## Screen 5: Turn 4 — Near Object Evaluation (This)
**Type:** Visual Demonstrative + Positive Sentiment (4s)

**UI Elements:**
- Avatar points to a sleek sports car parked right next to both characters with heart icons floating above it (😍)
- 4-second circular timer on the mic

**Audio & Mechanics:** Avatar asks: "Do you like this car?" User must synthesize the demonstrative and sentiment: "I love this car" or "I like this car".

---

## Screen 6: Turn 5 — Far Object Evaluation & Reduction (That)
**Type:** Distant Demonstrative + Spoken Reduction (4s)

**UI Elements:**
- Avatar points down the road to an old, rusted house with a red 'X' over it (👎)
- 4-second circular timer on the mic

**Audio & Mechanics:** Avatar asks: "What about that house over there?" User must produce: "I don't like that house" (/aɪ roʊn laɪk/) or "I hate that house".

---

## Screen 7: The Final Ear Trap (Surprise Check)
**Type:** Fast Acoustic Verification (3s)

**UI Elements:**
- Two rapid-tap buttons: [ Understand ] and [ Misunderstood ]

**Audio & Mechanics:** Avatar says goodbye at full street speed: "Cool, see ya later!" Text on screen displays: "How are you?" User has 3 seconds to tap [ Misunderstood ] to prove their ears aren't hallucinating matches under cognitive fatigue.

---

## Screen 8: Mastery Scorecard & Progression Gate
**Type:** Performance Audit & Gate Unlock

**UI Elements:**
- Real-time radar chart tracking:
  - Response Speed (Average latency under 2.5s)
  - Pronunciation Accuracy (≥80% match on keywords)
  - Conversational Flow (No unprompted hesitations)
- Large dynamic badge: UNIT 1 MASTERED or NEEDS REMEDIATION

**Audio & Mechanics:**
- Pass (≥80% total score): Celebratory audio cue, awards XP, permanently unlocks Unit 2
- Fail (<80% score): Identifies the exact broken turn and routes learner to a mandatory 60-second recovery drill before allowing a re-attempt.

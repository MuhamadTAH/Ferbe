# Lesson 1.3: Rapid Speaking (Theme A: Social Ping-Pong)

**Objective:** Force unassisted vocal output under descending timers. Transition from listening to reflexive spoken responses without reading text.
**Duration:** ~2.5 minutes (8 screens)

---

## Screen 1: Shadowing Warm-Up (Untimed Rehearsal)
**Type:** Guided Articulation

**UI Elements:**
- Clean text card: Hey!
- Large central mic button with audio waveform display

**Audio & Mechanics:** Native audio models the open, relaxed vowel: "Hey!" Mic activates. Learner repeats with zero time pressure. Visual waveform confirms vocal volume and pitch before moving forward.

---

## Screen 2: Echo Drill — The Question Frame (Untimed Rehearsal)
**Type:** Intonation Mimicry

**UI Elements:**
- Text banner: How are you?
- Pitch curve diagram indicating rising-falling conversational intonation

**Audio & Mechanics:** Audio models natural linking: /haʊərjuː/ ("How are you?"). User taps mic and records. App validates basic acoustic clarity (untimed, up to 2 retries allowed).

---

## Screen 3: Visual Status Trigger (Tier 1 Speed: 5s)
**Type:** Cued Speech Production

**UI Elements:**
- Visual cue: Big thumbs-up icon
- Scaffolded prompt: I'm [ ? ], thank you.
- 5-second circular timer on the mic button

**Audio & Mechanics:** Audio prompt asks: "How are you?" Learner must read the cue and vocalize: "I'm good, thank you" before the timer expires.

---

## Screen 4: Status Shift (Tier 1 Speed: 5s)
**Type:** Lexical Swap Under Pressure

**UI Elements:**
- Visual cue: Sunglasses icon (cool)
- Text prompt: I'm [ ? ], thank you.
- 5-second timer

**Audio & Mechanics:** Audio prompt: "How are you?" Learner speaks: "I'm cool, thank you" within 5 seconds. Enforces instant substitution inside the frame.

---

## Screen 5: The Bounce-Back Trigger (Tier 2 Speed: 4s)
**Type:** Question Reversal

**UI Elements:**
- Graphic: Curved return-arrow icon
- Sub-label: Ask them back!
- Text scaffold: What about you?
- 4-second timer

**Audio & Mechanics:** Avatar audio plays: "I'm fine, thank you." User must immediately speak the counter-question: "What about you?" into the mic within 4 seconds.

---

## Screen 6: Two-Part Assembly (Tier 2 Speed: 4s)
**Type:** Chained Spoken Output

**UI Elements:**
- Visual cue: Stars/Celebration icon (great)
- Scaffold: I'm great, thank you... [ ? ]
- 4-second timer

**Audio & Mechanics:** Avatar speaks: "Hey! How are you?" User must produce the complete two-part phrase: "I'm great, thank you, what about you?" within 4 seconds.

---

## Screen 7: Social Greeting Trigger (Tier 3 Speed: 3s)
**Type:** Fast Reflex Handshake

**UI Elements:**
- Avatar appears wearing a nametag: Ahmad
- Zero text scaffolds
- 3-second countdown ring

**Audio & Mechanics:** Audio cue chimes. Learner must greet the specific person out loud before countdown ends: "Hi Ahmad" or "Hey Ahmad".

---

## Screen 8: Cold Ping-Pong Sprint (The Speaking Gate: 3s)
**Type:** Unassisted Spoken Capstone

**UI Elements:**
- Avatar waves in real-time
- Red 3-second countdown ring around live mic icon
- Zero text or hints

**Audio & Mechanics:**
- Avatar speaks native audio: "Hey! How are you?"
- Mic opens immediately with strict 3-second countdown
- Learner must spontaneously produce: "I'm good/cool/fine, thank you, what about you?"

Passing unlocks Theme B (Lesson 1.4: Opinions & Distance). Misfires route to a single untimed retry loop.

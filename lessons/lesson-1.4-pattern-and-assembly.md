# Lesson 1.4: Pattern & Assembly (Theme B: Opinions & Distance)

**Target:** This / That, objects (car, flower, house), opinion gradient (love, like, don't like, hate), and the agreement chunk (Me too).
**Duration:** ~2.5 to 3 minutes (8 screens)

---

## Screen 1: Spatial Anchor (This vs. That)
**Type:** Concept Deconstruction (Distance)

**UI Elements:**
- Two side-by-side cards:
  - Card 1 (Near): Hand touching a car up close → This (ذس / ئەمە - نزیک)
  - Card 2 (Far): Hand pointing at a car down the street → That (ذات / ئەوە - دوور)

**Audio & Mechanics:** Tapping each card plays crisp audio. User must tap both to see the distance animation and enable Continue.

---

## Screen 2: Concrete Object Anchors (car, flower, house)
**Type:** Lexical Anchor Pairing

**UI Elements:**
- 3 photo cards with bold English text:
  - car (کار / ئۆتۆمبێل)
  - flower (فڵاوەر / گوڵ)
  - house (هاوس / ماڵ)

**Audio & Mechanics:** Audio plays "flower". User has 4 seconds to tap the flower card. Audio plays "house" → user taps the house card. Pairs sound directly to spelling and visual meaning.

---

## Screen 3: Demonstrative Snap (This / That + Noun)
**Type:** Tactile Slot-and-Filler

**UI Elements:**
- Visual: Image of a house right next to the user
- Sentence slots: [ ___ ] [ house ]
- Tile options: [ This ], [ That ]

**Audio & Mechanics:** User drags This into the blank slot. Once snapped, engine plays connected native audio: "This house."

---

## Screen 4: The Opinion Ladder (love, like, don't like, hate)
**Type:** Gradient Sentiment Sorting

**UI Elements:**
- 4 interactive sentiment cards with icons:
  - love (😍 / ئای ڵەڤ)
  - like (👍 / ئای ڵایک)
  - don't like (👎 / ئارۆن ڵایک)
  - hate (😡 / ئای هەیت)

**Audio & Mechanics:** User taps each card to hear its emotional prosody. Learner arranges them in order from strongest positive to strongest negative.

---

## Screen 5: Sentence Frame Construction
**Type:** Syntax Assembly

**UI Elements:**
- Image: A sleek sports car up close with a heart icon
- Incomplete frame: I [ ___ ] this car.
- Choices: [ love ], [ hate ], [ how ], [ are ]

**Audio & Mechanics:** User selects love. Full sentence locks in, audio plays "I love this car," and a Kurdish translation tag flashes briefly below.

---

## Screen 6: The Agreement Trigger ("Me too")
**Type:** Pragmatic Reaction Matching

**UI Elements:**
- Speech bubble from avatar: "I like BMW, what about you?"
- Two reaction buttons:
  - [ Me too ] (می توو / منیش)
  - [ I'm fine ] (ئام فاین)

**Audio & Mechanics:** User taps [ Me too ]. Audio confirms with an energetic "Me too!" Reinforces natural conversational agreement.

---

## Screen 7: Untimed Shadowing Rehearsal
**Type:** Oral Articulation Practice

**UI Elements:**
- Full sentence display: I hate that car.
- Waveform visualization box with central microphone button

**Audio & Mechanics:** Native audio plays with clear, stressed intonation on hate. User taps mic and records with zero timer penalty. Waveform confirms volume and clarity.

---

## Screen 8: Assembly Gate (Timed Synthesis: 5s)
**Type:** Timed Construction Check

**UI Elements:**
- Visual cue: An old, broken-down car parked far away with a red 'X'
- 4 jumbled tiles: [ that car. ] [ I ] [ don't like ] [ this ]
- 5-second countdown timer

**Audio & Mechanics:** Learner must tap tiles in correct sequence: I → don't like → that car. Completing the frame before the clock runs out clears Lesson 1.4 and unlocks Lesson 1.5.

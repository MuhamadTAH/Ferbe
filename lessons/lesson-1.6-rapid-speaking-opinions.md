# Lesson 1.6: Rapid Speaking (Theme B: Opinions & Distance)

**Objective:** Force unassisted vocalization of spatial demonstratives (This / That), opinion scales (love, like, don't like, hate), and instant agreement (Me too) under descending countdown timers.
**Duration:** ~2.5 minutes (8 screens)

---

## Screen 1: Shadowing Warm-Up (Untimed Rehearsal)
**Type:** Guided Articulation (The Reduction)

**UI Elements:**
- Clean text card: I don't like
- Pronunciation cue: /aɪ roʊn laɪk/ (ئارۆن ڵایک)
- Central microphone button with active waveform monitor

**Audio & Mechanics:** Native audio models the reduction at conversational speed: "I don't like." Learner taps mic and records with zero time pressure. Waveform confirms fluid linking before unlocking Screen 2.

---

## Screen 2: Demonstrative Vocal Contrast (Untimed Rehearsal)
**Type:** Articulatory Spatial Contrast

**UI Elements:**
- Two prompt cards:
  - Near: This car (/ðɪs kɑːr/)
  - Far: That car (/ðæt kɑːr/)

**Audio & Mechanics:** Audio plays both in sequence. User taps mic and records both phrases back-to-back: "This car... That car." System checks vowel distinction (/ɪ/ vs. /æ/) without penalties.

---

## Screen 3: Reflex Agreement (Tier 1 Speed: 5s)
**Type:** Conversational Echo

**UI Elements:**
- Speech bubble from avatar: "I like Dolma, what about you?"
- Scaffold cue: Say you agree!
- 5-second circular countdown timer on the mic button

**Audio & Mechanics:** Prompt audio plays smoothly. Mic activates immediately. Learner must vocalize: "Me too!" within 5 seconds.

---

## Screen 4: Distance + Object Naming (Tier 1 Speed: 5s)
**Type:** Visual Demonstrative Trigger

**UI Elements:**
- Visual: Hand pointing at a house far down the road
- Scaffold text: [ That ] [ house ]
- 5-second timer

**Audio & Mechanics:** Audio chime rings. User must look at the distance cue and speak: "That house" into the mic.

---

## Screen 5: Positive Opinion Sprint (Tier 2 Speed: 4s)
**Type:** Sentiment Frame Production

**UI Elements:**
- Visual: Sleek BMW up close with a heart icon (😍)
- Incomplete text prompt: I [ ? ] this car.
- 4-second countdown timer

**Audio & Mechanics:** Audio prompt asks: "Do you like this car?" Learner must speak: "I love this car" within 4 seconds.

---

## Screen 6: The Spoken Reduction Under Pressure (Tier 2 Speed: 4s)
**Type:** Negative Contraction Output

**UI Elements:**
- Visual: Broken-down car right next to the user with a thumbs-down icon (👎)
- Prompt: Say you don't like it!
- Scaffold: I don't like... [ ? ]
- 4-second countdown timer

**Audio & Mechanics:** User must speak with smooth connected speech: "I don't like this car" before the timer elapses.

---

## Screen 7: Strong Negative Rejection (Tier 3 Speed: 3s)
**Type:** High-Intensity Emotional Output

**UI Elements:**
- Visual: An ugly house far away with an angry emoji (😡)
- Zero text scaffolds
- Red 3-second countdown ring

**Audio & Mechanics:** Audio asks: "What about that house?" User must produce: "I hate that house" within 3 seconds.

---

## Screen 8: Cold Capstone Gate (The Speaking Gate: 3s)
**Type:** Unassisted Spoken Capstone

**UI Elements:**
- Avatar points to a fresh flower right in front of the screen with a heart icon (😍)
- Center 3-second countdown ring around live mic
- Zero text, hints, or audio models

**Audio & Mechanics:**
- Avatar asks: "What do you think of this flower?"
- Mic opens instantly with strict 3-second limit
- Learner must produce: "I love this flower" or "I like this flower"

Passing unlocks Lesson 1.7 (The Boss Checkpoint). Failing routes to a 45-second targeted remediation loop on demonstratives and opinion verbs.

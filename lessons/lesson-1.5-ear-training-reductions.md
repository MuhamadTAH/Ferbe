# Lesson 1.5: Ear Training & Reductions (Theme B: Opinions & Distance)

**Objective:** Train the ear to decode natural American connected speech—specifically the reduction of "I don't like" into colloquial speech (ئارۆن ڵایک)—and acoustically differentiate near vs. far demonstratives (This vs. That) at native speed.
**Duration:** ~2.5 minutes (8 screens)

---

## Screen 1: Acoustic Reality Check (Robot vs. Native)
**Type:** Connected Speech Perception

**UI Elements:**
- Two playable waveform buttons:
  - [ 🤖 Robot Mode ]
  - [ ⚡ American Street Mode ]

**Audio & Mechanics:**
- Robot Mode plays rigid citation audio: "I — do — not — like — this — car."
- Street Mode plays natural rapid reduction: /aɪ roʊn laɪk ðɪs kɑːr/ (ئارۆن ڵایک ذس کار)
- System prompts: "Which one matches real native speech?" User taps Street Mode.

---

## Screen 2: Sentiment Decoder by Ear (Like vs. Don't Like)
**Type:** Auditory Polarity Discrimination

**UI Elements:**
- Two large icon cards: [ 👍 Like ] and [ 👎 Don't Like ]. Zero text.

**Audio & Mechanics:** Fast audio plays: "I don't like this car." Learner has 4 seconds to tap [ 👎 Don't Like ]. Next clip: "I like this car." → learner taps [ 👍 Like ].

---

## Screen 3: The Distance Trap (This vs. That)
**Type:** Minimal Acoustic Distinction

**UI Elements:**
- Two visual scenario cards:
  - Card A: Hand touching a car up close
  - Card B: Hand pointing at a car far down the street

**Audio & Mechanics:** Audio plays native speed: "I love that car." User must isolate the vowel contrast (/ðɪs/ vs. /ðæt/) purely by ear and tap Card B (Far) within 4 seconds.

---

## Screen 4: Blind Lexical Identification
**Type:** Auditory Target Extraction

**UI Elements:**
- Waveform player with question: "What did the speaker say?"
- 3 English text options:
  - [ I don't like that flower ]
  - [ I don't have that flower ]
  - [ I hate this flower ]

**Audio & Mechanics:** Audio plays rapid connected speech: "I don't like that flower." User must correctly identify the full transcribed phrase from audio alone.

---

## Screen 5: Agreement Ear Trap (Me too vs. Distractors)
**Type:** Conversational Reaction Discrimination

**UI Elements:**
- 3 playable audio response bubbles: [ 🔈 Response A ], [ 🔈 Response B ], [ 🔈 Response C ]

**Audio & Mechanics:**
- Prompt plays: "I like Dolma, what about you?"
- Response A plays: "Me too."
- Response B plays: "You too."
- Response C plays: "Thank you."
- Learner taps Response A within 4 seconds.

---

## Screen 6: Intensity Discrimination by Pitch & Stress
**Type:** Prosodic & Emotional Decoding

**UI Elements:**
- 3 emotion cards: [ ❤️ Love ] [ 👍 Like ] [ 😡 Hate ]

**Audio & Mechanics:** Audio plays with heavy vocal friction and downward stress: "I hate that house!" User taps [ 😡 Hate ]. Next audio plays melodic, elevated pitch: "I love this house!" → user taps [ ❤️ Love ].

---

## Screen 7: Blind Tile Assembly (Ear-to-Syntax)
**Type:** Syntactic Reconstruction from Sound

**UI Elements:**
- Audio play button at top. Zero text visible.
- 4 scrambled word tiles: [ this car. ] [ don't like ] [ I ] [ hate ]

**Audio & Mechanics:** Audio plays fast native reduction: "I don't like this car." Learner taps tiles in order: I → don't like → this car. The distractor tile [ hate ] remains unused.

---

## Screen 8: The Speed Reduction Gate (Checkpoint: 3s per rep)
**Type:** Rapid-Fire Auditory Evaluation

**UI Elements:**
- Center 3-second countdown ring
- Rapid binary choice: [ Match ] or [ Mismatch ]

**Audio & Mechanics:**
- Rep 1: Audio "I don't like that car." On-screen: car far away with red 'X'. → Tap Match
- Rep 2: Audio "I love this flower." On-screen card: a house. → Tap Mismatch
- Rep 3: Audio "Me too." Screen text: "You too". → Tap Mismatch

Score ≥2/3 unlocks Lesson 1.6 (Rapid Speaking: Opinions & Distance).
